Quantum Computing Milestones Reached in 2026

Physicists have successfully demonstrated fault-tolerant logical qubits operating continuously above ninety-nine point nine percent fidelity in neutral-atom quantum processors.

This landmark achievement paves the way for scalable quantum error correction routines capable of simulating complex molecular interactions previously impossible on classical supercomputers.

Multiple academic institutions and private laboratories collaborated to confirm the coherence times across sustained runtime intervals spanning several hundred milliseconds.
